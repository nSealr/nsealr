YOUR KEY STAYS OFFLINE

Use your signer

The companion connects your Nostr client to the device. Keep recovery words, private keys, passphrases and share backups on the device or in your offline backup.

Load your identity

Use the device on battery power, with both USB cables disconnected. Choose Load a key, or generate recovery words from the camera or dice under Tools. Keep the backup offline.

In the companion, choose Read from device, scan the request with the device, then scan its response. You can scan a request before loading its key; choose Use identity after checking the npub to resume it. Compare the full public identity before you continue.

Review a request

  1. Ask your client to sign, or write a note in the companion.
  2. Scan the displayed QR with the device. Keep scanning until all frames arrive.
  3. Check the action, identity, targets and text on the device. Open exact details when needed. Approve or reject there.
  4. Choose Scan device response. Point the camera at the device QR until every frame is captured.

The companion checks the returned event and signature. Publishing is a separate action; a valid signature alone does not mean a relay accepted the note.

Connect a client

For a Nostr website, install the browser extension and reload that website. Check its origin before allowing access. Each key operation still needs the device.

For a remote client, choose a secure relay and Open session. Use the pairing link in the client, then confirm its displayed key. Keep the companion open. Reloading or closing it ends that session.

Private messages

Set your message inbox, then choose one recipient. The device reviews the message and recipient before encryption. To read a selected message, approve its verification and plaintext release on the device. Once released, the client can store that text.

Shared identities

Create a group with three devices and sign with two. Load the public group record into the companion; secret share backups stay offline. Two surviving participants can repair a lost share while keeping the same npub. This does not revoke a stolen copy.

Follow the setup and recovery guide before starting a group ceremony.
